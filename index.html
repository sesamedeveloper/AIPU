What should you know before starting?

  • Basic familiarity with JavaScript and web development is helpful.
  • Some exposure to MERN is useful, but your skill level can vary.
  • Be curious, ask questions and be willing to try things out.

  • Build your skills by working on practical projects and using AI throughout the development process.

AIPU is not about skipping the fundamentals or handing all your decisions to AI. It's about building on your current skills and learning to use AI responsibly throughout the development process.

Detailed answers to common questions about the AIPU programme, learning tracks, project work, and requirements.

AIPU is an AI-enabled development programme that focuses on applying MERN skills through a structured software-development assignment. Rather than concentrating only on learning tools or generating code, it emphasises using AI throughout the development journey, reviewing its output and demonstrating the resulting work.

You'll work through a structured project journey involving requirements, planning, design, development, debugging, testing, review and documentation. The required work products and completion criteria form part of the programme.

You'll work on a structured, real-world software-development assignment informed by software-company practices. The exact project, scope and working arrangements will be explained before enrolment. Do not interpret this as a promise that every participant will work on a live customer engagement.

The programme focuses on AI-assisted work across the development process — including understanding requirements, exploring solutions, implementation, debugging, testing and documentation. You remain responsible for reviewing and verifying AI-generated output.

Your intended evidence of learning includes your implementation, required project artefacts, documentation and final demonstration. Completion depends on meeting the programme's assessment and handover criteria.

It is designed for freshers, junior developers and early-career professionals with relevant web-development foundations. The learning track should reflect your current level and readiness.

You should have basic familiarity with JavaScript and web-development concepts, along with some exposure to MERN. Confirm the final admission prerequisites and self-assessment criteria with the programme team.

The programme is focused on learning to use AI in development. Confirm the final entry requirements with the team; advanced AI expertise should not be presented as a prerequisite unless the admission criteria require it.

The programme is intended to help you apply your current skills through a structured project journey. Your learning track determines the expected pace and degree of independence.

The programme is organised around a structured learning and project process. Before enrolment, the programme team will outline the guidance arrangements, review frequency and communication channels available to learners.

You will need to work through the problem systematically — investigating the issue, exploring possible solutions and validating your approach. The programme provides a confirmed process for requesting mentor support and resolution.

Mentoring and review sessions are aligned with your selected learning track delivery model. Specific one-to-one reviews and feedback touchpoints are structured to help you refine your project deliverables.

Evaluation is based on the programme's defined deliverables, assessments and final demonstration. Clear assessment milestones ensure learners understand what is expected at each stage of the project.

The current learning-track structure is 20 days for advanced learners, 40 days for intermediate learners and 60 days for beginners, subject to self-assessment placement.

AIPU is offered through flexible formats including online interactive sessions and hybrid mentoring. Contact our admissions team to confirm current batch availability and location schedules.

Learners typically dedicate 3 to 5 hours daily for project execution, AI collaboration exercises, and review sessions, with flexible timings accommodated for working professionals.

AIPU uses a self-assessment to help determine the appropriate learning track. The final placement process and any possibility of changing tracks are confirmed together with the programme advisors.

Certificate eligibility depends on meeting the programme's required deliverables, assessments, final demonstration, handover and approved completion criteria. Completing registration or attending alone does not guarantee a certificate.

Programme fees depend on your evaluated learning track (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced). Enquire with our admissions advisors for exact fee breakdowns, payment terms, and early enrolment benefits.

Our team will help you understand the programme, clarify your current skill level and answer your questions about eligibility, learning tracks, duration, format and fees. You can then decide whether AIPU is the right next step.
